Loss function and training protocol

Total loss formulation

L = (LC + LD) + Lode + wsm Lsmooth
  • LC, LD: Mean squared error evaluated exclusively at real report dates (no interpolation artifacts).
  • Lode: Autograd relative ODE residuals over Nf = 200 collocation points:
    Σ [(e' − Tfe)/e]2 + [(i' − Tfi)/i]2 + [(c' − Tfc)/c]2 + [(d' − Tfd)/d]2
  • Lsmooth: Mean squared derivative Σ (dβ/dτ)2.

Why relative ODE residuals?

An outbreak grows exponentially across orders of magnitude. Under standard absolute residuals, early-stage errors contribute negligible loss. The E6 ablation reveals absolute residuals yield 75%–128% error vs. < 0.2% under relative scaling.

Two-stage optimiser

  • Stage 1 — Adam: LR 2×10−3 with cosine annealing, 2000 iterations. Resamples random collocation points per step to escape local minima.
  • Stage 2 — L-BFGS: Strong-Wolfe line search (2500–6000 iters) on a fixed linspace grid for quadratic convergence.
  • Float64 precision: Strict double precision prevents rounding errors during higher-order autograd differentiation.
  • Ensemble: 5 random seeds; median trajectory acts as robust point forecast.

Forecasting protocol

Hold β at its empirical 14-day trailing mean; freeze inferred μ; propagate SEIRD forward via DOP853 numerical solver.

Ablation insights (E6)

Adam-only: e(t) error jumps to 96%. Physics weight wode = 10: errors triple. Fourier features reduce β RMSE by 28%.

Key design decision: The L-BFGS second-order refinement is indispensable. Adam alone establishes coarse trends, but fails to resolve stiff ODE phase trajectories.
