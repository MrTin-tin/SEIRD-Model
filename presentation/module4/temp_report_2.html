3. Loss Function and Training Protocol

3.1 Composite Loss Formulation

The multi-objective loss function balances data fidelity, differential dynamics, and functional regularity:

L = LC + LD + Lode + wsm Lsmooth

where empirical terms are computed over discrete surveillance dates τk:

LC = (1/nC) Σ [c(τk) − Ck/K]2,   LD = (1/nD) Σ [d(τk) − Dk/K]2

The physics loss is evaluated over Nf = 200 interior collocation points using reverse-mode automatic differentiation:

Lode = (1/Nf) Σ [ ((e' − Tfe)/e)2 + ((i' − Tfi)/i)2 + ((c' − Tfc)/c)2 + ((d' − Tfd)/d)2 ]
Lsmooth = (1/Nf) Σ (dβ/dτ)2

Here K represents the normalization scale (peak cumulative cases in training). Hyperparameters are fixed at wdata = wode = 1, with smoothness weight wsm = 10−4 for synthetic experiments and 10−3 for real outbreak data.

Remark on Relative vs. Absolute Residuals: In epidemic outbreaks, caseloads expand across orders of magnitude. Under canonical absolute residuals (x' − Tf)2, the early phase where (e, i, c) are small contributes practically zero loss gradient. The network fits only the peak, resulting in massive early dynamical drift. Normalising by state magnitude yields scale-invariant loss contributions across the entire trajectory.

3.2 Two-Stage Optimization Protocol

Optimization follows a disciplined two-stage sequence implemented entirely in IEEE float64 precision:

  1. Stage 1 — Stochastic Exploration (Adam): Initial learning rate of 2×10−3 with cosine annealing over 2,000 iterations. Resampling interior collocation points each iteration injects stochastic noise that escapes flat plateaus and aligns coarse trajectory envelopes.
  2. Stage 2 — Deterministic Refinement (L-BFGS): Quasi-Newton optimization using a strong-Wolfe line search over 2,500–6,000 iterations evaluated on a fixed linspace grid. The second-order curvature approximation resolves stiff ODE couplings and converges to tight gradient tolerances.

Point forecasts are constructed from an ensemble of 5 independent seeds. For out-of-sample forecasting, β is held constant at its trailing 14-day mean, combined with the learned CFR parameter μ, and propagated via DOP853.
