DUAL-NETWORK ARCHITECTURE

Anatomy of the Neural Architecture

  • 1
    State Network g_θ(τ): Takes rescaled dimensionless time τ = (t - t₀) / T ∈ [0, 1] and predicts the 4 state variables (e, i, c, d) normalized by peak scale K. 3 hidden layers (48 units each, tanh).
  • 2
    Transmission Network h_φ(τ): A dedicated second MLP that maps time to continuous transmission rate β(τ) > 0 through a shifted Softplus activation.
  • 3
    Continuous Collocation Sampling: Instead of a discrete grid, the network samples random continuous interior timepoints {τⱼ} ~ U(0, 1) at each step, testing physical law satisfaction everywhere.
  • 4
    Two-Stage Hybrid Optimization: Stage 1: Adam with Cosine Annealing (2,000–6,000 iters) navigates complex landscapes; Stage 2: Deterministic Quasi-Newton L-BFGS with Strong Wolfe line search locks into machine-precision convergence.

Why Standard PINNs Fail on Epidemics & Our 6 Architectural Fixes

Embedding physical mass conservation, hard initial conditions, and scale normalization directly into the network architecture.

STRUCTURAL INVARIANTS

Exact Mass Invariants & Hard Boundaries

  • 1
    Observable Transformation (C = I + R + D): Because dC/dt = σ E, we eliminate R and S: R = C - I - D and S = N - E - C. Mass conservation S + E + I + R + D ≡ N holds IDENTICALLY for any weights—zero loss penalty needed!
  • 2
    Hard Boundary Initial Conditions: Setting x(τ) = x₀ + τ · g(τ) guarantees x(0) = x₀ exactly at τ = 0 to machine precision. Eliminates initial condition hyperparameter tuning!
  • 3
    Multiplicative Positivity Embeddings: Exponential heads e = e₀ exp(τ g₁) keep states strictly positive while growing across 4 orders of magnitude; Softplus guarantees β(τ) > 0 unconditionally.
GRADIENT BALANCING

Identifiability & Numerical Conditioning

  • 4
    CFR Bounding (Eliminating Spurious Basins): Bounds Case-Fatality Ratio in [20%, 80%] via sigmoid logit. Blocks the pathological basin where μ → 0 and I → ∞ to match deaths!
  • 5
    Fourier Features (Defeating Spectral Bias): Embeds time with sin(π k τ), cos(π k τ) (k=1..4). Enables the network to capture sharp intervention drops and resurgences that plain tanh smooths away.
  • 6
    Relative Residual Formulation: Dividing residuals by states (r_e / e) prevents peak epidemic errors from starving early-outbreak gradients, ensuring 1% error means 1% at day 5 or day 150.
